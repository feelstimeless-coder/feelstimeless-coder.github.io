Signature itineraries

Sample trips to steal ideas from

Every itinerary we send is custom — these samples show the level of detail you can expect. Tap one open.

7 days · Gorillas & the Wild West — Bwindi + Queen Elizabeth

Day 1: Kampala → Lake Mburo, sundowner game walk. Day 2: Equator crossing, on to Bwindi through the terraced hills. Day 3: Gorilla trekking — your hour with a mountain gorilla family. Day 4: Community visit, transfer to Queen Elizabeth NP. Day 5: Dawn game drive, afternoon Kazinga Channel boat cruise (hippos, elephants, hundreds of birds). Day 6: Ishasha's tree-climbing lions, begin return. Day 7: Breakfast stop at the Equator, back to Kampala.

6 days · Kenya Duo — Nairobi city + Diani beach

Day 1: Fly to Nairobi, giraffes at the Giraffe Centre, dinner out. Day 2: Nairobi National Park morning drive, Maasai market, SGR or short flight to the coast. Day 3–5: Diani: villa or beach resort, reef snorkeling day, dhow sunset cruise, seafood night. Day 6: Lazy morning, fly home tanned.

10 days · The Grand East Africa — Kampala → Serengeti → Zanzibar

Days 1–2: Kampala and Jinja — source of the Nile, optional rafting. Days 3–5: Fly to the Serengeti via Kilimanjaro; two full days among the migration herds. Days 6–9: Zanzibar: Stone Town heritage walk, spice farm, north-coast beaches, final-night dhow dinner. Day 10: Fly home with a full camera roll.

3 days · Weekend reset — Jinja adventure or Ssese slow-down

Pick your pace. Adrenaline: rafting the Nile, quad biking and a riverside camp bonfire. Or serenity: the ferry to the Ssese Islands, forest walks, hammocks and lake sunsets. Both leave Kampala Friday, return Sunday night.

Questions

Asked all the time

How do payments work?

We confirm your trip with a deposit (usually 30%), with the balance due before departure. We accept mobile money, bank transfer and card. Every payment gets a receipt, and your quote is locked once paid.

Can I book for my whole company or staff?

Absolutely — it's one of our specialties. Use the booking form to add every traveler (there's a "Staff member" option), or send us a list and we'll handle rooming, transport and dietary needs in one plan with one invoice.

What if I don't know where I want to go?

That's the fun part. Tell us your budget, dates and the feeling you're after — rest, adventure, romance — and we'll pitch you three itineraries to choose from. No charge for dreaming.

Do you handle visas and travel insurance?

Yes. We guide you through visa applications for your destination, prepare supporting documents, and arrange travel insurance so you're covered from take-off to touchdown.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Life happens. Most trips can be rescheduled free of charge with enough notice; cancellation terms depend on airlines and lodges, and we always explain them clearly before you pay.

When is the best time to visit Uganda?

Uganda is a year-round destination, but the dry seasons — December to February and June to September — are best for gorilla trekking and savannah game drives. The green seasons bring fewer crowds, lush scenery and better lodge rates. Tell us your month and we'll tell you what shines then.

How do gorilla trekking permits work?

Permits for Bwindi and Mgahinga are issued by the Uganda Wildlife Authority, are limited per day and sell out months ahead in high season. We check live availability, secure your permit and build the rest of the itinerary around your trekking date.

Is East Africa safe for travelers?

Millions of travelers visit Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania safely every year. We use vetted guides, drivers and lodges we know personally, plan sensible routes and stay reachable on WhatsApp throughout your trip — plus we arrange travel insurance for every itinerary.
