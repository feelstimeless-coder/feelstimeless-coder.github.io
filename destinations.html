Timing your trip

Best time to visit East Africa

There's no bad month — only different magic. Here's the honest cheat sheet our planners use.

Dec – Feb

Dry & golden. Prime gorilla trekking in Bwindi, superb game viewing in Murchison and the Mara, and Zanzibar at its sunniest. Book permits early.

Mar – May

Green season. Dramatic skies, lush landscapes, newborn wildlife and the best lodge rates of the year. Fewer crowds on every trail.

Jun – Sep

Peak safari. The driest trails for trekking, the wildebeest migration in the Serengeti–Mara, and perfect beach weather on the coast.

Oct – Nov

Short rains & festivals. Quieter parks, great value — and Nyege Nyege lights up Jinja in November. Our favourite insider window.
