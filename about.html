The people behind the feeling

We're a small, obsessive team of planners, guides and storytellers who believe travel should be effortless for you and unforgettable forever.

Our story

Born online. Raised on the road.

Feels Timeless started as a group of friends organizing trips for other groups of friends — a villa on the coast here, a safari there. Word spread. Today we're a modern travel company based in Nakawa, Kampala, Uganda, planning journeys across East Africa and beyond for individuals, couples, families and entire companies.

We meet you where you are — WhatsApp, a call, a video chat, or coffee in Kampala. No office overheads baked into your price, planners who answer at 9pm when inspiration strikes, and itineraries you can poke at until they're perfect.

  • Registered in Uganda, serving travelers across East Africa and beyond.
  • Vetted partners — every lodge, driver and guide is one we've used ourselves.
  • Transparent pricing — one quote, no ambush fees at the airport.
