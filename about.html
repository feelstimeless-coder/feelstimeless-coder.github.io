The people behind the feeling

We're a small, obsessive team of planners, guides and storytellers who believe travel should be effortless for you and unforgettable forever.

Our story

Born online. Raised on the road.

Feels Timeless started as a group of friends organizing trips for other groups of friends — a villa on the coast here, a safari there. Word spread. Today we're a fully online-based travel company operating from Nakawa, Kampala, planning journeys across East Africa and beyond for individuals, couples, families and entire companies.

Being online-based is our superpower: no shiny office overheads baked into your price, planners who answer at 9pm when inspiration strikes, and itineraries built in shared documents you can poke at until they're perfect.

  • Registered in Uganda, operating online from Nakawa, Kampala.
  • Vetted partners — every lodge, driver and guide is one we've used ourselves.
  • Transparent pricing — one quote, no ambush fees at the airport.
What we stand for

Three promises on every trip

Local at heart

We're East African, and it shows — in the hidden beaches we know, the guides we trust and the prices we negotiate.

We don't chase checklist tourism. We build slow, rich journeys designed to still feel special in twenty years.

Care you can feel

Birthday on day three of your safari? There will be cake. We sweat the small stuff so you don't have to.
